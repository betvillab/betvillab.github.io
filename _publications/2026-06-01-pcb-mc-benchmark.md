We introduce **PCB-MC**, a benchmark dataset designed to support research in missing component detection on printed circuit boards (PCBs). This dataset enables standardized evaluation of computer vision and deep learning methods for industrial quality inspection, addressing the lack of publicly available, annotated PCB inspection datasets.

**Presented as a poster at:** Netherlands Conference on Computer Vision (NCCV 2026) — June 2026.
