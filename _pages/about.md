I am a PhD Candidate in Computer Vision at the [University of Twente](https://www.utwente.nl), working in the [Data Management & Biometrics (DMB)](https://www.utwente.nl/en/eemcs/dmb/) group under the supervision of [Dr. Estefanía Talavera](https://estefaniatalavera.github.io/). My doctoral research focuses on developing deep learning and computer vision algorithms for **automated defect detection in industrial assembly processes** — bridging the gap between academic machine learning and real-world manufacturing quality control.

Before joining UT, I worked as an Automation & Digital Transformation Engineer at [Gunnebo Safe Storage](https://www.gunnebo.com), where I designed and deployed visual inspection systems for assembly lines and developed Industry 4.0 solutions including IoT, data acquisition, and machine learning pipelines. I continue this industry collaboration as an Industrial Transformation Engineer (Global), driving AI-based inspection systems and digitalization roadmaps across global manufacturing sites.

My background is in **Mechatronics Engineering** (BSc, Universidad Autónoma del Caribe, Colombia) and **Mechatronics & Micromechatronic Systems** (MSc, Universidad de Oviedo / Karlsruhe University of Applied Sciences — *Erasmus Mundus Joint Master Scholarship*).

## Research Interests

- **Computer Vision for Industrial Inspection** — automated visual quality control in manufacturing environments
- **Deep Learning for Defect Detection** — robust ML pipelines for missing component and defect classification
- **Real-Time Vision Systems** — low-latency inference integrated into production lines
- **Visual Quality Control** — data-driven quality assurance and manufacturing traceability

## Current Projects

**VIAS — Visual Inspection Assembly System** *(Gunnebo Safe Storage)*  
Designed and deployed a computer vision system for automated inspection of assembly processes. Integrated machine learning algorithms for defect and missing-object detection with real-time monitoring and data-driven quality analysis.

**PCB-MC: A Benchmark Dataset for Missing Component Detection in Printed Circuit Boards**  
Building a benchmark dataset to support research in missing component detection — *conference paper in progress*.

**Computer Vision in Manufacturing for Missing Object Detection: A Survey**  
Comprehensive survey of state-of-the-art CV methods applied to industrial missing object detection — *manuscript in progress*.
