I am a **PhD Candidate in Computer Vision** at the [University of Twente](https://www.utwente.nl) (Netherlands), working in the [Data Management & Biometrics (DMB)](https://www.utwente.nl/en/eemcs/dmb/) group under the supervision of [Dr. Estefanía Talavera](https://estefaniatalavera.github.io/). My research focuses on building deep learning and computer vision systems for **automated defect detection in industrial assembly processes** — making manufacturing smarter, faster, and more reliable.

Alongside my PhD, I work as an **Industrial Transformation Engineer (Global)** at [Gunnebo Safe Storage](https://www.gunnebo.com), driving Industry 4.0 and AI-based inspection initiatives across global manufacturing sites.

My background spans **Mechatronics Engineering** (BSc, Universidad Autónoma del Caribe, Colombia) and **Mechatronics & Micromechatronic Systems** (MSc, Universidad de Oviedo / Karlsruhe University of Applied Sciences — *Erasmus Mundus Joint Master Scholarship*).

## 🛠 Current Projects

**VIAS — Visual Inspection Assembly System** *(Gunnebo Safe Storage)*  
End-to-end computer vision pipeline for automated quality inspection of assembly lines. Integrates real-time defect and missing-object detection with data-driven quality analytics.

**PCB-MC: Missing Component Detection in Printed Circuit Boards**  
Annotated benchmark dataset to support standardized evaluation of computer vision methods for missing component detection in printed circuit boards. *Accepted at EUVIP 2026 — to be presented in Luxembourg.*

**Survey: Computer Vision in Manufacturing for Missing Object Detection**  
Systematic review of state-of-the-art methods, datasets, and open challenges for CV-based missing object detection in industrial settings. *Accepted in IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics (IEEE TII).*
